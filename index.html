5. Domain 1: Security and Risk Management

🧠 BRAIN DUMP
  • ▸Policy hierarchy: policy → AUP → baselines → guidelines → procedures; new safeguard = new baseline
  • ▸Risk plans: strategic (5-yr, updated annually), tactical (1-yr), operational (monthly–quarterly)
  • ▸Risk responses: accept, mitigate/reduce (accept residual), transfer/assign (insurance, outsourcing), avoid, deter — rejection = risk is unacceptable
  • ▸Risk types: inherent (before controls), residual (after), total (no safeguards)
  • ▸Quantitative = objective, 6 steps incl. inventory & cost-benefit; qualitative = subjective, uses Delphi technique (anonymous consensus)
  • ▸Threat modeling: focus on asset / attacker / software; diagramming + reduction analysis (trust boundaries, data-flow paths, input points, privileged operations)
  • ▸Frameworks: STRIDE (Microsoft, software), PASTA (7 stages, asset value), VAST (agile), DREAD (damage, reproducibility, exploitability, affected users, discoverability), Trike (acceptable risk)
  • ▸NIST SP 800-37 RMF = 7 steps: Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, Monitor — OCTAVE / FAIR / TARA are real-world frameworks, NOT the NIST RMF
  • ▸COBIT = IT governance framework, 5 principles, light exam coverage
  • ▸Control categories: technical / administrative / physical
  • ▸Control types: deterrent, preventive, detective (mandatory vacation, job rotation, honeypots), compensating, corrective (AV, backups), recovery (clustering, hot sites), directive (signage, policies)
  • ▸Supply-chain assessment: on-site visits, document exchange, policy review, third-party audits; also delayed loss after an event
